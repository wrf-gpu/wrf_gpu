Boundaries & Known Issues

v0.3.1 — WRF-order fixes and measured performance

On RTX 5090 (sm_120, CUDA 13) with an AMD Ryzen 9 9950X host, the cached two-domain Canary 24-hour forecast takes 3.604 s per forecast hour through process completion, initialization and output included. The original CPU-WRF twelve-core main-loop reference is 92.3 s/hour: 25.6× the stated rate ratio. GPU wall time is 12.6% lower than v0.3.0's 4.126 s/hour. Completed 24-hour original CPU comparisons pass the frozen bounds on Canary, Tenerife 0227 and Swiss January/February. Swiss January passes D6 and direct RC3 strict integrity on all 25 frames in each domain. A separate six-hour WN3 sweep measured N=4 at 7.77 s/case-hour (15.9× CPU throughput), including init/load and without compression; no energy or longer-run throughput is extrapolated. See current evidence and exact clocks.

wrf_gpu is deliberately explicit about its edges. Nothing here is a hidden gap: every unsupported namelist selection is refused before any compute with a named reason, and every open item is tracked with a proof object. This page is the honest map of what is and is not claimed.

What is NOT claimed¶

  • Validation scope is the 24 h window on the tested configuration. The release gate covers 6 Tenerife cases × 3 domains × 24 h: 375 output fields compared with original CPU-WRF, 62 of them with frozen per-variable limits (D6), 0 bound failures, all compared values finite. Beyond 24 h, a complete six-case comparison on an earlier internal build of v0.3.0 (identical except the Noah-MP albedo/snow-aging fix) exceeds those limits for some 1 km wind/rain fields in two of the six 72 h cases; every affected frame is listed in the validation report. Station verification is done separately by the ALISIOS forecasting project.
  • Single GPU only. MPI and multi-GPU forecasts are unsupported; independent forecasts can share one card. Tested on RTX 5090 (sm_120, CUDA 13); other recent NVIDIA GPUs are untested.
  • Not a universal WRF v4. Standard regional ARW configs and the common operational subset only. Every other scheme is classified (reference-only-with-oracle or documented-boundary) and fails closed with a named reason. See Physics → Scheme triage.
  • Broad 24 h/72 h forecast-skill equivalence is open — the credibility gate. v0.3.0 passes its 24-hour six-case Tenerife release gate and v0.23.4 its nine-nest gates, but those do not establish seasonal/configuration-independent equivalence. See Validation.
  • Some accepted dynamics controls are path-specific. The nested path honors moist_adv_opt/scalar_adv_opt, but the single-domain daily path currently runs 0/0. Omitting time_step_sound also selects 10 acoustic substeps in the runtime where pristine WRF derives 4 on the tracked fixture; residual dry-mass behavior is unresolved.
  • Single-card speedup is modest and scale-dependent — the GPU is host/launch-bound and slower than CPU on tiny geometries; ≥2–3× is not single-card reachable and fp32 cannot move it. See Performance.
  • Multi-GPU throughput is projected, not measured (one physical RTX 5090 here). No per-watt / per-kWh at-scale claim; no head-to-head competitive benchmark.
  • fp32 is the default since v0.3.0, validated on the 24 h release gate (beyond 24 h see the disclosures above). Two-way nesting is finite/stable but its 24 h equivalence is untested. K2 multi-GPU is experimental / default-off / lab-only (periodic-BC bit-exact, specified-BC not yet faithful — not for production).
  • Not WPS, DFI, FDDA / grid / obs / spectral nudging, WRF-Chem, WRF-Fire, WRF-Hydro, urban (BEP/BEM) or lake (the last two are reference-only / fail-closed). Aerosol-coupled Morrison (mp=40) and NSSL fail closed.

The extreme-terrain ceiling (~6000 m)¶

The Alpine / Mont-Blanc extreme-terrain boundary instability is fixed (WRF-faithful zero-gradient physical W + specified-boundary cadence, no masking). The remaining ceiling is ~6000 m: a nest over ~8000 m terrain (Karakoram) still diverges to a non-finite state — which the default-on finite guard catches and fails closed (0 bad frames written), and on which WRF's own real.exe likewise fails without a lowered etac. The deep > 6000 m fix is a future dycore-boundary milestone; the validated production envelope is steep real-world terrain up to ~6000 m.

Free-running open-boundary stability¶

Free-running without lateral-boundary relaxation on wide domains (nx ≈ 160+) can go unstable beyond ~14 h. The validated operational path uses boundary forcing (KI-7). See Dynamical Core → Boundaries.

Out-of-memory behavior¶

The co-resident-headroom OOM mode is solved by the launch-time VRAM preflight (fail-closed exit 75). The solo cuda_async fragmentation mode is mitigated, not fixed: a bit-identical RRTMG column-tile cap cuts the radiation transient's largest allocation −37 %, but solo fragmentation can still OOM the full fp64 nest, and the long-horizon 9-nest can OOM around the ~90 min integration horizon (a reproducer ships). The default-on finite guard aborts cleanly with {domain, field, level, step, sim-time, index} rather than propagating a corrupted state. No OOM-proof guarantee is made.

Roadmap to a complete port¶

Every item that is currently reference-only, fail-closed, or out-of-scope — every WRF v4 scheme not yet wired (including the 3-D turbulence closures km_opt=2/3/5 = 3-D TKE / 3-D Smagorinsky / SMS-3DTKE), plus data assimilation, coupled models, and output/grid gaps — is enumerated with a per-item "how to close it" in the port-completion roadmap. Its explicit goal is to drive the reference-only and fail-closed columns to zero. You can regenerate the live status any time with python -m gpuwrf.cli namelist-support (see Running the Model). A code-task inventory also lives in docs/GPU_PORT_GAPS_TODO.md.
