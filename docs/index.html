wrf_gpu User's Guide

Regional forecasts on one NVIDIA GPU

Your guide to installing wrf_gpu and running a WRF-compatible forecast.

wrf_gpu rewrites WRF v4's ARW dynamics and physics for JAX/XLA/Pallas. Start with prepared WRF inputs and a supported namelist, run time integration on your GPU, and use familiar NetCDF tools to read the history files.

Get your first forecast running¶

1. Install
Check Python, CUDA JAX, your NVIDIA GPU and WRF runtime tables.
2. Run your first forecast
Try the bundled 3 km Alpine case, then inspect its output.
3. Configure your case
Select the duration, fixed nests, physics and output directory.
4. Read your results
WRF history, full output, cadence and checkpoint files.

Prefer help from an AI coding assistant? Follow the AI quickstart. The repository's operator runbook uses the same public commands.

Already have a WRF case?¶

Bring namelist.input, wrfinput_d0* and wrfbdy_d01. Keep your preprocessing and downstream analysis. For fixed nests, use --domains-from-namelist. The input guide explains preparation and running the model shows the commands.

Choose your configuration¶

Check the physics menu, namelist compatibility and nesting guide for your case. Options have explicit support status and unsupported selections stop with a named reason. Technical boundaries and current source-specific findings are on the limitations page.

See the evidence and plan your workload¶

Original Fortran CPU-WRF comparisons cover selected regional forecasts through 24 and 72 hours. Validation links every retained hourly error plot, its source and frozen limits; it distinguishes accepted scope from source-dated candidate findings. Universal observational-skill equivalence is not claimed.

Measured regional workloads show substantial speed gains on one RTX 5090. See performance and energy for the exact hardware, cases, clock definitions and dated receipts. Independent forecasts can share the card.

Read how the rewrite works or the release history when you want more detail. This independent implementation is not affiliated with UCAR/NCAR; see credits and licensing.
