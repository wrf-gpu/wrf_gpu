## Parallel 24-hour sweep

N=4 warm WN3 three-nest cases 0227/0502/0614/0220 ran the shipped launcher,
fused C-auto and retained autotune settings. Whole aggregate is 5.507
s/case-forecast-hour (22.4× CPU's stated 123.4 s/case-hour reference). The
all-four-running steady window is 4.857 (25.4×). Per-case init/load and history
are included; there is no compression. The measured durations differ from the
old v0.3.1 six-hour sweep and must not be treated as a pure code comparison.

[Sweep summary](evidence/v032/sweep24_summary.json) preserves admission,
resource peaks, timestamped energy and 75/75 0227 determinism. Warm per-PID
VRAM is 5,032 MiB and reported host high-water about 5.6 GB/case.

## Energy accounting

E158 timestamp-trapezoid board energy is 0.678 Wh (2.441 kJ)/case-hour [M]; the
528-second covered span accompanies the sweep receipt. Headline energy does
not subtract idle. The 66.67 W GPU host share is inferred as 4/12 of the

board plus host 0.780 Wh (2.808 kJ) [I]. CPU energy is 200×123.4/3600 =
6.86 Wh (24.68 kJ) [I]. Power boundaries are not asserted as package or
whole-node measurements. Sampling is retained in the producer summary.
