v0.3.1 — WRF-order fixes and performance (2026-10-05)

Open-top and per-record boundary-mass fixes, WRF edge-wind order, Andreas snow surface exchange, nested initial-state blending, thin-snow/ground-precipitation and warm-ice corrections, water/glacier history parity. A1-SL and Noah-MP column kernels are defaults; warm admission and three-nest C-auto are repaired.

Measured Canary 24-hour rate: 3.604 s/forecast hour (RTX 5090, AMD Ryzen 9 9950X), versus v0.3.0 4.126. Original CPU-WRF twelve-core reference: 92.3 s/hour. Swiss January D6 and direct RC3 strict integrity pass all 25 frames in each domain. Evidence, clocks and limits.

A release-by-release summary. stability > identity is the top priority, but a proven correctness repair may intentionally change results and require a rebaseline, as v0.23.4 and v0.3.0 do. Full per-release notes live in the release_notes/ folder of the repository.

Reading the "default numerics" column

Byte-identical = same compiled program, bit-for-bit output vs the prior release. Numerically equivalent = value-preserving change to the program (proven CPU bit-identical + within the GPU autotune floor). New capability is almost always opt-in and inert by default.
