Dynamical Core

wrf_gpu integrates a nonhydrostatic, split-explicit ARW dynamical core on the GPU — the same architecture as WRF's Advanced Research WRF core, re-implemented in JAX and validated cell-for-cell against CPU-WRF as the oracle. The dynamics/thermodynamics core is proven cell-for-cell identical to CPU-WRF v4; the priority order in every design decision is stability > identity > speed > memory, with no masking, clamping, or nan_to_num on the result path.

Time integration: RK3 + acoustic sub-stepping

The core uses WRF's split-explicit time scheme. A third-order Runge-Kutta (RK3) large step advances the slow (meteorologically significant) modes, while the fast acoustic and gravity-wave modes are integrated with smaller acoustic sub-steps inside each RK3 stage. Only rk_order=3 is supported.

Flux-form advection

Prognostic variables are advanced with flux-form advection. The advection orders are frozen to the WRF real-data default (5th-order horizontal, 3rd-order vertical). Optional moisture flux-advection into RK3 is available and gated:

KeyAccepted valuesNote
moist_adv_opt0 (standard), 1 (positive-definite), 2 (monotonic)Opt-in; default-off = byte-identical. WENO variants (3/4) not wired.
scalar_adv_opt0 / 1 / 2As above.

Active flux-form moisture advection makes WRF's RQVFTEN available, which the Tiedtke and New-Tiedtke cumulus schemes require (see Physics Options).

Default-off means byte-identical

The moisture/scalar flux-advection levers are opt-in and default-off; with them off the forecast is byte-for-byte identical to the baseline. They move toward the open 24 h forecast-skill gap but do not close it (see Validation).

fp64 by default

The dynamical core runs in fp64 by default. Double precision is needed around the pressure-gradient / buoyancy cancellation, where the difference of two large, nearly-equal terms would lose accuracy in single precision. The fp64 path is the validated, byte-identical default.

fp32 is opt-in and capability-only

An opt-in mixed-precision fp32 mode exists, but it buys VRAM (−14.4%) and cell capability (~1.16×), not single-card speed (throughput ratio ≈0.91), and its fidelity is verified only at the 1 h lead. The default remains pure fp64. See Performance.

Horizontal diffusion

The port implements and operationally wires two horizontal-diffusion closures; the 3-D closures fail closed with a transition recipe:

ClosureSettingStatus
Constant-Kdiff_opt=2, km_opt=1 (or diff_opt=0)Operational
2-D Smagorinsky (horizontal)diff_opt=1, km_opt=4Operational — WRF's recommended real-data default; includes terrain-slope and map-factor deformation terms (WRF-formula parity)
3-D TKE / 3-D Smagorinsky / SMS-3DTKEkm_opt=2 / 3 / 5Fail-closed — not implemented

If your namelist uses a 3-D closure (km_opt=2/3/5), switch to constant-K diff_opt=2 / km_opt=1 (vertical mixing then comes from the PBL scheme). Other numerics knobs: diff_6th_opt 0/2 (2 = monotonic 6th-order filter, no up-gradient flux), damp_opt 0/3 (3 = upper-level w-Rayleigh damping), and w_damping 0/1.

Gravity-wave drag

Orographic gravity-wave drag with flow blocking (gwd_opt=1) is wired and default-ON on the nested 1 km path. It requires the sub-grid orography statics in wrfinput. Set GPUWRF_GWD_NESTED=0 to force it off for a memory-tighter config. The GSL drag suite (gwd_opt=3) is not wired.

Lateral boundaries and free-running stability

Limited-area runs are forced through lateral-boundary relaxation from the boundary tendencies (wrfbdy for the root; the parent supplies each nested child's boundary live — see Nesting). This is the validated operational path.

Free-running open boundaries can go unstable (KI-7)

Running free (no lateral-boundary relaxation) on wide domains (nx ≈ 160+) can go non-finite beyond approximately forecast hour 14. The validated operational path uses boundary forcing; keep boundary relaxation on for wide-domain runs.
